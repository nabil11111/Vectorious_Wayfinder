## What every piece shares

- **An order's status** moves forward as each role does its part: `draft` → `placed` → `planned` or
  `deferred` → `loaded` → `delivered` → `received`. A deferred order keeps the date the shop wanted and goes
  to the front of the next plan. A split order is two orders linked to the original (D-17).
- **Counts follow the goods.** Ordered, loaded, delivered and received quantities are recorded per order line
  at each step, so a shortage shows where it happened.
- **A plan** is one depot's delivery day, `draft` then `published`. A trip goes `planned` → `loading` →
  `ready` → `out` → `done`.
- **A problem** is one kind of record whoever raises it (a loader's flag, a refused delivery, a short
  receipt), and the dispatcher decides each one in one place.
- **The clock.** Every time a person sees comes from the app's one clock, never from the device (D-18).
- **Live updates.** A change another role should see is announced, and the screens that care fetch it (D-21).
- **Every endpoint** is under `/api/v1` with its shapes in `packages/contracts`. It checks the role and that
  the record belongs to the caller's outlet or depot. Anything two people can change carries a revision.
  Anything sent from a phone carries an id made on the phone, so a retry is never counted twice.
- **Tables** are added by the piece that needs them. One person writes the tables and the migration before
  the builders start.

## The cut line

If we fall behind, pieces go in this order: A8, then the extras in A7 (the district map), then A9. Push
notifications, sounds, Sinhala and Tamil and a real road map are out unless time is left.

Never cut: all four roles working end to end, the rule checker, a suggested plan that explains its deferrals,
the driver working offline, and the README walkthrough on a fresh install.

Whatever we build differently from the design goes into the README under "Departures from the design". Known
so far: sign-in uses a username and password where the design shows a staff ID and PIN, there is no language
choice yet, and the demo clock control is ours.
