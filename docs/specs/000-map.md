## What every piece shares

**The life of an order.** One status moves forward as each role does its part:

`draft` → `placed` (shop) → `planned` or `deferred` (dispatcher sends the plan) → `loaded` (loader marks the
truck ready) → `delivered` (driver) → `received` (shop)

A deferred order keeps the date the shop wanted, its reason and how many times it has waited, and it goes to
the front of the next plan. An order that does not fit can be split: the part that fits goes out and the rest
waits. Each part is an order of its own, linked to the original, and is planned or deferred whole (D-17).

**Counts follow the goods.** Ordered, loaded, delivered and received quantities are recorded per order line
at each step, so a shortage shows exactly where it happened. They are not squeezed into the status.

**A plan** is one depot's delivery day: `draft` → `published`. A change after publishing raises the plan's
revision, and the loader and driver are shown what moved and why.

**A trip** goes `planned` → `loading` → `ready` → `out` → `done`. **A stop** goes `pending` → `arrived` →
`delivered`, `part delivered` or `not delivered`.

**A problem** is one kind of record whoever raises it: a loader's flag, a refused or failed delivery, a short
receipt. It goes `open` → `decided` → `closed`, and the dispatcher decides each one in one place.

**The clock.** Every date and time comes from the app's one clock, never from the device. The demo day sets
it and so do the tests (D-18).

**Live updates.** A change that another role should see is announced on one live stream, and the screens
that care fetch the new data straight away. A slow timer is the backup (D-21).

**Every endpoint** lives under `/api/v1` with its request and response shapes in `packages/contracts` and one
error shape. It checks the role, and that the record belongs to the caller's own outlet or depot. Anything
two people can change at once carries a revision number, and a stale change is refused with a clear message.
Anything sent from a phone carries an id made on the phone, so a retry is never counted twice.

**Tables.** Reference data, orders and plans exist already (`docs/data-model.md`). Loading checks, problems,
deliveries, proof and receipts are added by the piece that needs them. One person writes the tables and the
migration before the builders start.

## The cut line

If we fall behind, pieces go in this order: A9, then A8, then the extras in A7 (the district map on the
dashboard). Push notifications, sounds, Sinhala and Tamil and a real road map are out unless time is left.

Never cut: all four roles working end to end, the rule checker, a suggested plan that explains its deferrals,
the driver working offline, and the README walkthrough on a fresh install.

Whatever we build differently from the design goes into the README under "Departures from the design". Known
so far: sign-in uses a username and password where the design shows a staff ID and PIN, and there is no
language choice yet.
